# Set the prior

**State the outcome. Put a number on it. Write both down before you build.**

One sentence for the outcome, in the user's terms, not the system's. One number
for your confidence, between 0 and 1.

## Why a number and not a word

"High confidence" cannot be wrong. A number can. That is the whole point: it
commits you to something the evidence can contradict, and it makes the posterior
at the end an update rather than a new opinion.

Measured over one day: this gate was reached 37 times, went N/A zero times, and
produced a number three times. A gate that can never fail to apply and almost
never produces its artifact is not running.

## What counts

- **FIRED** — the outcome is stated in one sentence AND a number is recorded.
- **BLOCKED** — you are past the point where the prior was owed. Say so; do not
  backfill a number and call it a prior.
- **N/A** — legal only when nothing is being built, fixed or shipped.

## The tell

If you find yourself writing a posterior and cannot point at the prior it
updates, this gate did not fire, whatever the row says.
