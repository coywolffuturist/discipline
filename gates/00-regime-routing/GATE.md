## The read

**What KIND of problem is this, and therefore how deep does the work go?**

| regime | what it looks like | mode |
|---|---|---|
| **clear** | the method is known and the answer is checkable | **fast-track** — do it, verify, stop |
| **complicated** | one right answer exists but needs expertise to find | **standard** — the full suite, one pass |
| **complex** | cause and effect are only clear afterwards | **probe** — a cheap reversible experiment FIRST, then decide |
| **chaotic** | it is on fire and nothing is stable | **stabilize** — act to restore footing; method comes after |
| **disorder** | you cannot tell which of the four you are in | **halt** — say so and ask. Disorder answered confidently is the most expensive state on record |

## Why this is NOT the tiering that was refuted

The conductor refuses tiers, and the reason is exact: **a tier decides which
gates fire, and one scalar field cannot carry that** — `cold-read` exists as a
skill, an agent and a hook at once. That refusal stands, unchanged.

This gate does not touch which gates fire. **Every gate still fires or is N/A
against its own written trigger, in every regime, including fast-track.** What
the regime sets is the DEPTH of the work between the gates: how many options
gate 07 must name, whether gate 09's refutation is run before or after building,
whether gate 18 needs an independent refuter or an honest BLOCK.

Depth of work is not the same axis as coverage of gates. The refuted design
shortened the checklist. This one shortens the investigation and leaves the
checklist whole.

## The trigger

Fires when the same request could honestly be answered at more than one depth —
which is most work worth ranking in ka123n.

**N/A** when the step is a single named action with one obvious method: a file
move, a lint run, a lookup with a known door. Cite that in the row.

## The failure this exists to catch

**Disorder answered as if it were clear.** Every expensive failure in the record
has this shape: a confident method applied to a problem nobody had classified.
A wallet balance was "derived" four times and corrected four times, always by
him. A demand claim was published from the worst ordering of a flow. Both were
complex problems worked in fast-track. Neither needed a bigger checklist — they
needed someone to say *this is a probe, not an answer.*

**Halt is a legal outcome of this gate**, and it is the one that will be
skipped. Naming disorder costs a sentence. Not naming it has cost days.
